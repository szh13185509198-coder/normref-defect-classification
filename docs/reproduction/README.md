# Reproduction archive

`supplementary_full_record.md` is the complete, uncompressed supplementary record of the paper (about 35,000 words).
The submitted Supplementary Material is a compressed version of it; everything removed from the submitted version is kept here unchanged:

- launch records, interruptions and re-launches of every evaluation round, and the table of main result files with SHA-256 (record S2.3–S2.4);
- the development BA of every variant of every selection round (record S4);
- the clock-policy, power-mode and per-line tables of the Jetson measurements (record S8);
- the full development and formal robustness grids (record S11.3–S11.4), the 18 backbone configurations, per-product evidence purity and distance tables (record S12.4, S12.7, S12.8);
- the Real-IAD data table, the contrasts with a nominal class and the per-object differences (record S13.2, S13.4, S13.5);
- the per-product values of every hypothesis (record S14.2–S14.3, S15.2);
- the U2 / U2-A controls, the U4 (reflection-padding) geometric tables, sensitivity coefficients, per-product geometric effects, per-session latency and energy windows (record S16).

Table and section numbers inside the record are those of the uncompressed version. Every released result file and its SHA-256 are listed in `RESULTS.md`.
